# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Bestie to be a welcoming, practical, and safety-minded open-source project. Contributors should be able to collaborate without harassment, discrimination, or abuse.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but be clear about technical risks.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Avoid sharing private data, secrets, or personal conversations.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Hate speech or discriminatory language
- Sexualized comments or unwanted attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberately derailing issues, PRs, or discussions
- Encouraging unsafe use of Bestie or unsafe handling of user data

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or ban participants who violate this code of conduct.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.
